Star models and re-apply them from a star button in the composer.

## What you get

- A **star action** in thread, queued-message, and side-chat composers that
  opens your starred models plus the thread provider's full catalog, with
  star/unstar controls on every row.
- One click sets the thread's model for the next turn — no message sent, no
  active work restarted.
- A **Model favorites** section in the plugin's settings to star models from
  any provider with a reasoning level.

## How it works

Favorites are stored per provider and model in the plugin's own storage on
the BB server. A thread keeps the provider it was created with, so favorites
for other providers are shown but cannot be applied to that thread.
